Product checklist (so your ToS is enforceable)

Explicit checkbox at sign-up: “I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.”

Both links open in new tabs; no pre-checked boxes.

Record consent: user id, terms version, IP, timestamp, user-agent.

Store in user_consents (or on profiles) with terms_version.

Versioning: When Terms change, force re-consent on next login; store the new version + timestamp.

Email a receipt of the Terms link on sign-up (“Welcome to My Pup—here are the Terms you agreed to on [date]”).

In-flow notice: show the short disclaimer near any high-risk actions (bookings, boarding, grooming, payouts).

Insurance & safety (optional but powerful):

Let providers attest to having liability/animal-bailee coverage; collect policy info.

Provide an optional incident-report form and escalation email.

Offer optional background checks with a clear “no guarantee” notice.
