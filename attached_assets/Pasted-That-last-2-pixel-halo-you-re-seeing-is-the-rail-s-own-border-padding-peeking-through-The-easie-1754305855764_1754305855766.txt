### TASK: make the active blue pill fully fill the rail (zero gap)

SCOPE
• Edit ONLY client/src/pages/Marketplace/Tabs.tsx

STRATEGY
• Keep the rounded rail with 1 px border.
• For each trigger add a `before:` pseudo that becomes the blue background
  **only when the tab is active**.  
  Because `before:` is `absolute` + `inset-0`, it covers the trigger’s whole
  cell, hiding the rail padding/border completely.

STEPS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Rail container (unchanged from previous step)

```tsx
<Tabs.Root
  defaultValue="services"
  className="inline-flex overflow-hidden rounded-full
             border border-gray-300 bg-gray-50"
>
Trigger (apply to every tab)

tsx
Copy
Edit
<Tabs.Trigger
  value="services"
  className="
    relative flex-1 z-10   /* z-10 keeps icon/text above pseudo */
    flex items-center justify-center gap-2
    px-4 py-1.5 text-sm font-medium whitespace-nowrap

    text-primary-700          /* inactive */

    before:content-[''] before:absolute before:inset-0
    before:rounded-full before:bg-primary-600
    before:opacity-0
    data-[state=active]:before:opacity-100
    data-[state=active]:text-white
  "
>
  <BriefcaseIcon className="w-4 h-4" />
  <span>Pet Services</span>
</Tabs.Trigger>
Remove any bg-primary-600 you previously placed directly on the trigger; the pseudo now paints the background.

### Why this works
`before:absolute inset-0` stretches the pseudo-element to the exact interior
of the trigger, so no matter what padding/border the rail has, the blue layer
covers it completely. We keep text/icon above it with `relative z-10`.

Apply the same pattern to Pup Box and Store triggers and the pill will look
perfectly flush on every tab.
