What the screenshot shows
Rail – rounded pill with a thin gray border (border-2).

Active trigger – blue background, but its own border‐radius is slightly smaller and it still carries a 2 px white “ring” utility that Radix adds for focus (ring-offset-background ring-offset-2 ring-ring …).

Minimal change that removes the strip

You still have global focus styles everywhere else.
