Here’s what the original Supabase-linked Explore page looked like, with that breed ↔ color mapping baked right into the FilterBar:

Single header search
A full-width “Search puppies, breeds, or breeders…” input up top—no extra chrome.

Filter row immediately beneath

Breed dropdown: a <Select> showing all 50 breeds, each option prefixed by a tiny colored dot (e.g. Golden Retriever → gold, French Bulldog → light gray, Poodle → soft pink).

Color dropdown/swatches: right next to Breed, a “Color” filter. When you opened it, you saw all the coat colors we store in Supabase—Black, White, Brown, Golden, Brindle, Gray, Fawn, Merle, etc.—each rendered as a little circle filled with that exact hue.

Age (weeks), Gender, Verified only checkbox, and the Price slider all to the right of those two.

Popular breeds bar
Below the filters was a horizontal, scrollable row of clickable pill-buttons for the top 10–12 breeds. Each pill was tinted with its breed’s color (e.g. “Golden Retriever” pill had a gold background, “German Shepherd” pill was tan/black, etc.). Clicking one instantly applied both the breed and its color filter.

Listings grid
Your Supabase results then poured in—each card showing only the real listings (no “demo” badge), but if you were signed out you’d see the orange “Demo Data” badge instead.
