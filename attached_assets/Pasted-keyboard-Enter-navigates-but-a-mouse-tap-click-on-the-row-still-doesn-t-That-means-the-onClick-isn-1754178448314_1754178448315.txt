Why this works

<Link> is the official React-Router element; it handles click and tap by default.

No custom navigate() call needed—React Router swaps the URL instantly.

Keyboard support remains: when a row has focus, Enter activates the link automatically.

We still clear the query with onClick={() => { setQ(""); setI(-1); }} so the bar resets.
